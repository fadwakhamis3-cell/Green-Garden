Our Plants

Ornamental Plants

Ornamental plants are grown for decoration and beauty. They include flowering plants and plants with attractive leaves. These plants are commonly used in gardens, parks, and homes to improve appearance and create a pleasant environment.

Fruit Plants

Fruit trees are plants that produce edible fruits such as mangoes, oranges, and apples. They are important for food production and are widely grown in farms and nurseries. These trees need proper care, sunlight, and water to grow well.

Succulent and Cactus Plants

Succulent and cactus plants are types of plants that can store water in their leaves or stems. They are suitable for dry environments and require less water than other plants. These plants are easy to care for and are popular in homes and offices.
